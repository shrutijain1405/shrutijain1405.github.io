Why the geometry matters

A 9:16 crop keeps less than one-third of a 16:9 frame’s width.

  • The subject changes at every cut
  • Motion must remain stable
  • A small miss may result in missing the story

The narrower the canvas, the more intentional every decision must be.

Good framing is more than accurate tracking

The system must observe, judge, and refine—across motion, composition, and story.

Evaluation turns subjective quality into a repairable signal.

1  Observe  context

Read motion, composition, and story together.

2  Judge  quality

Name the failure: subject, framing, or temporal stability.

3  Refine  decision

Turn each verdict into the next repair—not just a score.

A good edit is coherent, readable, stable, and intentional.

Results

Building Auto Edit end to end

The work strengthened five capabilities I now bring to production AI systems.

Multimodal reasoning — translated creative intent and long-form video into structured, editable story plans.
Computer vision — built shot-aware grounding, tracking, and stable vertical framing around the subject that matters.
Production AI engineering — connected GPU-backed services through durable, parallel workflows with caching, fallbacks, and resumable state.
Evaluation and research — turned subjective editing quality into reproducible tests for framing, stability, latency, and cost.
Product ownership — carried the system from open-ended experiments to an integrated workflow with transparent human review.
