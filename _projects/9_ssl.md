This paper presents an ablation study of knowledge distillation in a semi-supervised setting, demonstrating that training a smaller student network on soft labels generated by a supervised teacher over unlabeled data not only reduces model size but also improves generalization beyond the supervised baseline — with a key finding that the fewer labeled examples available, the more the approach benefits from using a smaller student network, highlighting distillation as a practical and effective tool for semi-supervised computer vision in low-label regimes.
